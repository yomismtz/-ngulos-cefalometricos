Progressive workflow

In research, attempt the analysis before eligibility.

01

Define the protocol

Choose sample, ages, country/origin, groups, analyses and outcomes.

02

Open the radiograph

Common image formats and PDF are supported, including page selection for multipage documents.

03

Calibrate when required

Linear measurements in millimeters require calibration; angular geometry does not.

04

Attempt the landmarks

Only landmarks required by the selected measurements are requested.

05

Calculate and review QC

Warnings do not move landmarks or alter results automatically.

06

Finalize the case

Enter age, recorded sex, country and groups after tracing; YomCeph then evaluates eligibility.

07

Include or exclude with a reason

Missing landmarks, poor image quality, cropping, artifacts or other documented causes can exclude a case without deleting it.

08

Export research data

Excel, UTF-8 CSV and reproducible IBM SPSS syntax distinguish included, excluded and pending cases.

Research builder

Automatic criteria without silently discarding subjects.

Age and country

YomCeph generates inclusion/exclusion text from the protocol age range and country/origin. Researcher-defined criteria remain preserved alongside the automatic block.

Radiographic quality

Finalization can record poor image quality, unidentifiable landmarks, cropped anatomy, artifacts/superposition, unsuitable projection, duplicate/wrong radiograph or another reason.

Missing landmarks

If a required landmark cannot be identified safely, the case can be retained as excluded for auditability and is not counted in the included sample.

Configurable groups

No university, clinic or center is preloaded. Each study defines closed lists such as Group = Case | Control or Time = Pre | Post.

Scientific catalog

Only reproducible constructions are activated.

Available means explicit landmarks and geometry are implemented; raw values are kept separate from population references.

Available

Steiner · Downs · Tweed

Skeletal, dental and vertical blocks with explicit angular sectors and sign conventions.

Available

Craniocervical posture · Powell · Rocabado

Postural, soft-tissue profile, C0–C2 and hyoid measurements; calibrated linear measures require a scale.

Available

Wits · Björk–Jarabak · YEN · W

Published constructions covered by synthetic-coordinate tests.

Available

McNamara and 2D airway

A/Pg to N-perpendicular, Co–A, Co–Gn, differential, ANS–Me and descriptive pharyngeal widths. The facial axis belongs to Ricketts and uses Pt, not Ptm.

Available

Ricketts · reproducible core

Ba–N/Pt–Gn facial axis, facial/maxillary depth, mandibular plane, constructed Xi, lower facial height, mandibular arc, convexity, corpus and selected dental relations. VERT is not classified without an explicit age reference.

Available

COGS · Burstone

Hard-tissue block with a horizontal plane constructed 7° from SN, parallel/perpendicular projections, skeletal dimensions and dental relations.

Available

Sassouni · structural core

Four planes and pairwise divergence. Point O and the arc system remain non-automated until a sufficiently canonical computational construction is established.

Available

Bimler · verified core

F1–F5 and F7 factors, profile angle, basic angles and incisor relationships. F6/F8–F10 and the correlometer remain excluded rather than guessed.

Available

Sagittal G-triangle

Bo–X–K construction with signed AXK/BXK. Original reference ranges are from young Southern Chinese adults and are not treated as universal norms.

Reference

Alexander

The literature primarily describes the Alexander Discipline; no single canonical cephalometric analysis is assumed.

Different projection

Ritucci–Burstone

The published asymmetry method uses a submentovertex (SMV) projection, not one lateral cephalogram.
