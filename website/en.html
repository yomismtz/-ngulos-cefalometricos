Sample and eligibility

Study name, planned sample from 1 to 1000, optional future increase, age range and inclusion/exclusion criteria.

Cases outside the age range are marked NOT ELIGIBLE and excluded from included-case statistics.

Reproducible protocol

Analyses, outcomes, groups and reference sets are stored. After the first included case, the protocol is locked against silent methodological changes.

Separate databases

Individual cases never enter the study sample. Each research project maintains its own subjects and exports.

Scientific catalog
