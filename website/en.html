Progressive workflow

In research, attempt the analysis before eligibility.

01

Define the protocol

Choose sample, ages, country/origin, groups, analyses and outcomes.

02

Open the radiograph

Common image formats and PDF are supported, including page selection for multipage documents.

03

Calibrate when required

Linear measurements in millimeters require calibration; angular geometry does not.

04

Attempt the landmarks

Only landmarks required by the selected measurements are requested.

05

Calculate and review QC

Warnings do not move landmarks or alter results automatically.

06

Finalize the case

Enter age, recorded sex, country and groups after tracing; YomCeph then evaluates eligibility.

07

Include or exclude with a reason

Missing landmarks, poor image quality, cropping, artifacts or other documented causes can exclude a case without deleting it.

08

Export research data

Excel, UTF-8 CSV and reproducible IBM SPSS syntax distinguish included, excluded and pending cases.

Research builder

Automatic criteria without silently discarding subjects.

Age and country

YomCeph generates inclusion/exclusion text from the protocol age range and country/origin. Researcher-defined criteria remain preserved alongside the automatic block.

Radiographic quality

Finalization can record poor image quality, unidentifiable landmarks, cropped anatomy, artifacts/superposition, unsuitable projection, duplicate/wrong radiograph or another reason.

Missing landmarks

If a required landmark cannot be identified safely, the case can be retained as excluded for auditability and is not counted in the included sample.

Configurable groups

No university, clinic or center is preloaded. Each study defines closed lists such as Group = Case | Control or Time = Pre | Post.
